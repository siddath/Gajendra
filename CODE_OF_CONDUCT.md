# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community. Participate with empathy, assume good intent while addressing concrete impact, and keep criticism focused on ideas, code, and observable behavior.

Examples of constructive behavior include:

- giving specific, actionable feedback;
- respecting different experience levels, identities, and viewpoints;
- protecting contributors' privacy and using synthetic AI-agent metadata;
- accepting responsibility, apologizing when appropriate, and repairing harm.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, sexualized attention, threats, doxxing, publishing private AI conversations or credentials, sustained disruption, and personal attacks.

## Scope

This standard applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, release channels, and public spaces where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through [GitHub's private vulnerability-reporting channel](https://github.com/siddath/Gajendra/security/advisories/new), marking the report as a conduct matter. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and in good faith. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior threatens the community or project.

Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact, repair, and the safety of affected participants. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.
